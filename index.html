About CMWS

Congratulations! Your Viking Soul Cask has been reserved!

In autumn 2014, the first get-together of whisky clubs across Czech republic took place during the Whisky Life Prague festival.

This grouping proved very convenient when an email arrived from Orkneys in summer 2018 with this enjoyable message. With seven rapid-fire phone calls, the cask was distributed – and the CMWS was unknowingly born!
