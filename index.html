Purpose

A practical society for private-cask projects

The principal purpose of CMWS is to acquire private bottlings of Scotch whisky and coordinate the activities connected with selecting, purchasing and bottling those casks.

Our casks are selected directly from distilleries. Since 2022 the focus has been on whisky ready for direct bottling.
