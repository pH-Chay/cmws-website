Private casks selected to be enjoyed together.

CMWS brings together whisky clubs and enthusiasts around distinctive private-cask projects. Alongside the CMWS bottlings, the clubs behind the society also bring a long history of their own private selections.

Experience behind CMWS

Bottlings by participating clubs

Before and during the early years of CMWS, participating clubs had already selected and released numerous private-cask bottlings of their own.

This historical overview illustrates the experience the clubs brought into CMWS. It is not intended to be a complete record of club bottlings today.

The existing club-bottling list covers approximately the period up to 2021/2022. When added, it will also be shown from newest to oldest.
