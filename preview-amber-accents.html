About CMWS

It all started with an email from Orkney.

“Congratulations! Your Viking Soul Cask has been reserved!”

In autumn 2014, the first get-together of whisky clubs across the Czech Republic took place during the Whisky Life Prague festival.

This network came into its own when an email arrived from Orkney in the summer of 2018 with the message above. Seven rapid-fire phone calls were enough to share the cask among the clubs – and, without us quite realising it, CMWS was born!

Selection principles

Deliberately selective

01

Beyond the core

We are looking for distillery outliers. If you want the typical distillery profile, buy a core-range bottling!

02

From distilleries with love

Casks are selected directly from distilleries, even though this deliberately limits the available choice and makes negotiations more demanding.

03

Ready to enjoy together

Since 2022, CMWS has focused on mature whisky ready for immediate bottling, rather than speculative long-term cask ownership with an uncertain outcome.
