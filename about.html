About CMWS

A Czech society built around private-cask whisky.

CMWS was established in Prague in 2018 after collaborative private-cask projects demonstrated that Czech whisky clubs could achieve more by working together.

What we do

Private bottlings, selected with care

Today, CMWS brings together Czech whisky clubs to acquire distinctive private cask bottlings of Scotch whisky. We select our casks directly from distilleries and coordinate the process from tasting and selection through acquisition to bottling and official introduction to our clubs.

Since 2022, the focus has been on mature whisky ready for direct bottling.

The main purpose of CMWS is the acquisition of private Scotch whisky bottlings and the work surrounding them: identifying opportunities, tasting samples, selecting casks, negotiating with distilleries and coordinating bottling.

The Society deliberately chooses a demanding route. Casks are selected directly from distilleries rather than anonymously through the secondary cask market.

In 2022 another restriction was adopted: the focus would be on mature whisky ready for direct bottling.
